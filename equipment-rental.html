    FAQ

    Do the businesses you identify need to be actively for sale?

    No. We proactively contact business owners, including those who haven't formally considered selling or engaged a business broker.

    Can I focus on a specific geography or equipment category?

    Yes. Every search is built around your acquisition criteria, including geography, equipment specialization, company size, and other business characteristics.

    What if we've never acquired a business before?

    We work with businesses exploring their first acquisition as well as experienced acquirers looking to build a more consistent pipeline of opportunities.

    Will our acquisition search be confidential?

    Yes. We agree on an outreach approach with your team before contacting potential acquisition targets.

    Am I obligated to pursue the businesses you identify?

    No. You retain full control over which opportunities you explore and which businesses you ultimately acquire.

    Where do you want to grow?

    Whether you're considering your first acquisition, expanding into a neighboring market, or building a multistate rental operation, Hadley can help you find the businesses to get there.
