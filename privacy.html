Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

This policy describes how Hadley Inc ("Hadley", "we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you visit gethadley.com, contact us, or communicate with us by email, phone or text message.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us, such as your name, work email, phone number, company and the contents of messages you send us. We also keep business contact information about companies and their owners or principals from public and commercial sources, and records of our calls, emails and text messages with them. When you visit our website, we and our service providers collect standard technical information (such as IP address, browser and pages viewed) and may use tools that identify the company or business contact visiting.

How we use information

We use information to respond to you, to carry on business conversations about acquisitions, sourcing and our services, to schedule meetings, to operate and improve our services, and to comply with law. If you ask us not to contact you, we record that request and honor it.

How we share information

We share information with service providers that help us run our business (for example, hosting, email, telephony and messaging providers), only to perform services for us. We may share information with clients on whose behalf we contact you, or if required by law or to protect our rights. We do not sell personal information.

Text messaging

If you text one of our phone numbers, we will reply by text to continue the conversation you started. Message frequency varies with the conversation. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts from that number, and HELP for help, or contact us at the address below.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, excluding the aggregators and providers of the text message services.

Retention and security

We keep information as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and protect it with reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete information about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law.
